**Answer**

```
**First three steps (in this order) when a child is reported missing at MASAA_L2:**

1. **Contain the reporter and record the description.** Do not let the family member walk away to search. Record: name, age, height, clothing colours, footwear, distinguishing features, language spoken, wristband or group identifier, and the exact location and time last seen. [1, SOP-SEC-007]

2. **Raise the request immediately as LOST_PERSON P2** in the operations platform and broadcast the description on the sector radio channel. 3. **Seal the immediate area and search outward from the last known point.** Marshals at the zone's exits hold position and observe rather than joining the search. A child moves outward from where they were lost; staff who leave the exits to search inward let them past. [1, SOP-SEC-007]

Only after these three steps are complete does the wider search escalate. [1, SOP-SEC-007]

```
