# FAQ-001 — Multilingual Visitor FAQ / الأسئلة الشائعة للزوار

## 1. Purpose

Common visitor questions with the answers staff should give, plus the supported
language list used across the operations procedures. Where a question touches an
operational procedure, the governing document is named — staff answer from the
procedure, not from memory.

## 2. Supported languages

Service requests record the reporter's language from this list:

| Code | Language | Code | Language |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ar` | العربية (Arabic) | `fr` | Français |
| `en` | English | `ms` | Bahasa Melayu |
| `ur` | اردو (Urdu) | `fa` | فارسی (Persian) |
| `id` | Bahasa Indonesia | `bn` | বাংলা (Bengali) |
| `tr` | Türkçe | `ha` | Hausa |

Arabic and English are the mandatory announcement languages under
**SOP-CS-015**; the remainder follow.

## 3. Visitor questions

### "Where do I get water?" / "أين أجد الماء؟"

Hydration points are at every entrance and at intervals of no more than 150 m
along main routes. During hot periods, staff also distribute water inside busy
areas. If a station is empty, tell a marshal — it is logged and refilled under
**SOP-SAN-003**.

**نقاط المياه متوفرة عند كل مدخل وعلى مسافات لا تزيد عن ١٥٠ متراً.**

### "I have lost my child." / "فقدت طفلي."

Stay where you are and tell the nearest marshal immediately. Do not go looking
yourself — staff need you at the last known location to give the description,
and a searching parent frequently becomes a second missing person. The
description is broadcast within the first minute. Reunification points are
staffed at every site. Full procedure: **SOP-SEC-007**.

**ابق مكانك وأبلغ أقرب موظف فوراً. لا تبحث بنفسك.**

### "What are the evacuation procedures?" / "ما هي إجراءات الإخلاء؟"

If an evacuation is announced: move calmly in the direction the announcement
names, follow staff instructions, and do not turn back for belongings. Staff
move with the crowd and will direct you to the nearest open exit. Every site has
an assembly area. Announcements are made in Arabic and English first, then other
languages, and are kept short and repeated.

Evacuation is authorised jointly by the Duty Operations Director and the Civil
Defence Liaison. The full procedure, including triggers, routes per site, and
reopening conditions, is **SOP-CS-015**.

**إذا أُعلن الإخلاء: تحرك بهدوء في الاتجاه المذكور في الإعلان، واتبع تعليمات
الموظفين، ولا تعد لأخذ أمتعتك. الإخلاء يتطلب موافقة مشتركة من مدير العمليات
المناوب ومنسق الدفاع المدني. الإجراء الكامل موجود في الوثيقة SOP-CS-015.**

### "It is very hot — what should I do?" / "الجو حار جداً، ماذا أفعل؟"

Drink water before you feel thirsty, use shaded rest areas, and use the misting
fans when they are running. If you feel dizzy, stop and tell a marshal. Cooling
measures across the site are activated on a heat index, not on air temperature —
see **SOP-MED-009**.

**اشرب الماء قبل الشعور بالعطش، واستخدم مناطق الظل. إذا شعرت بدوار، توقف
وأبلغ أحد الموظفين.**

### "How long until someone comes to help?" / "متى تصل المساعدة؟"

It depends on the type of request. Life-threatening medical calls are
acknowledged within 4 minutes. Crowd-safety calls are the fastest at 3 minutes.
Lost-person reports are acknowledged within 10 minutes and worked continuously.
Routine requests such as signage or cleaning take longer by design, so urgent
calls are not delayed. Targets are in **SOP-OPS-001**.

### "This area is very crowded — is it safe?" / "المكان مزدحم جداً، هل هو آمن؟"

Zones are monitored continuously and staff act on defined thresholds well before
a zone becomes unsafe — inflow is throttled from 80% of capacity and stopped at
90%. If you feel pressure from the crowd, do not push back; move sideways toward
the edge of the flow and tell a marshal. Thresholds are in **SOP-CS-004**.

### "Where can I get a wheelchair?" / "أين أجد كرسياً متحركاً؟"

Ask any marshal or visit an information desk. Wheelchair and electric cart
services operate at all sites; a marshal will raise the request and stay with
you until it arrives.

### "I do not speak Arabic or English."

Show this card to any marshal. Staff will identify your language from the list
in section 2 and request an interpreter through the operations centre. Your
language is recorded on the request so the crew sent to you can be matched where
possible.

## 4. Staff guidance

- Answer from the procedure, and name the document when a visitor asks for the
  rule. "Inflow stops at 90%, that is SOP-CS-004" is a better answer than "it is
  safe, do not worry."
- Never estimate a response time better than the SOP-OPS-001 target.
- Never promise a reunification time. Give the process, not a deadline.
- If you do not know, say so and ask the SOC. A confident wrong answer sends a
  visitor in the wrong direction.
