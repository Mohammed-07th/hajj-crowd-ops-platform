# SOP-SAN-003 — Sanitation and Water Station Servicing

## 1. Purpose

Defines servicing intervals for water stations and sanitation facilities, and
the escalation path when a facility fails. Service request priorities and SLA
targets are in **SOP-OPS-001**: WATER is **P3 (30-minute response, 90-minute
resolution)** and SANITATION is **P4 (60 / 240)**.

## 2. Water station servicing intervals

| Condition | Servicing interval | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Normal operations | **every 4 hours** | Level, flow, cleanliness, cup supply |
| Zone at ELEVATED or HIGH utilisation | **every 2 hours** | As above |
| Heat Level 1–2 (WBGT 28–31.9 °C) | **every 2 hours** | As above, plus chilled-water availability |
| **Heat Level 3–4 (WBGT 32 °C and above)** | **every hour** | As above, plus roving distribution inside dense zones |
| Overnight (23:00–04:00), normal heat | every 6 hours | Level and flow |

**The baseline servicing interval for a water station is 4 hours.** It shortens
to 2 hours when the zone is busy or heat Level 1–2 is active, and to **hourly at
heat Level 3 and above** (see **SOP-MED-009**).

A servicing visit that finds a station below **25% capacity** shortens that
station's interval by half until two consecutive visits find it above 50%.

## 3. Sanitation servicing intervals

| Facility | Normal | Peak (zone at HIGH or above) |
|---|---|---|
| Toilet blocks | every 2 hours | every hour |
| Ablution areas | every 2 hours | every hour |
| Waste collection points | every 4 hours | every 2 hours |
| Litter sweep, primary routes | every 3 hours | every 90 minutes |

## 4. Escalation

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| Station empty or non-functional, normal conditions | WATER P3 request raised; refill within the 90-minute resolution target |
| Station empty during **heat Level 2 or above** | Raised **one priority higher** than the SOP-OPS-001 default — WATER becomes P2 |
| Two or more stations in the same zone out of service | Sector Supervisor informed; treated as **S3** under SOP-OPS-020 |
| Any station out of service at **heat Level 3 or above** | Immediate escalation to SOC Controller; mobile distribution deployed while the fix proceeds |
| Toilet block out of service in a zone at HIGH or above | S3 under SOP-OPS-020; alternate facilities signposted |
| Water quality concern | Station closed immediately, isolated, S2 under SOP-OPS-020 |

The priority-escalation rule in row two exists because a dry water station
during heat stress is a medical risk, not a facilities inconvenience.

## 5. Coverage standards

- Hydration points at every entrance and at intervals of no more than **150 m**
  along primary circulation routes (consistent with **SOP-MED-009** section 5).
- Toilet and ablution facilities within **250 m** of any point in an
  accommodation zone.
- Waste collection points within **100 m** of any food distribution area.

A zone falling below these standards for more than one shift is reported under
**SOP-OPS-020**.

## 6. Crew workflow

1. Servicing crews work a fixed route with a defined station list.
2. Each visit is recorded against the station identifier with a timestamp, even
   when no action was needed — an unrecorded visit is an unserviced station as
   far as the record is concerned.
3. Faults found during a routine visit are raised as service requests rather
   than fixed silently, so the failure appears in the metrics.
4. Route completion is confirmed at shift handover under **SOP-OPS-006**.

## 7. Heat-period surge

At heat Level 2 and above, servicing crews are augmented and roving distribution
begins inside dense zones. Pilgrims in a zone above 80% utilisation frequently
cannot reach a fixed hydration point even when one is close, so water is brought
to them. Roving distribution is coordinated with the Zone Marshal so that
distributors do not themselves create a stationary obstruction that raises local
density.
