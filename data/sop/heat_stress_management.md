# SOP-MED-009 — Heat Stress Management

## 1. Purpose

Defines the WBGT thresholds at which cooling measures are activated, how crowd
density interacts with heat risk, and what each activation level requires.

## 2. WBGT

Heat stress is assessed using the **Wet Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT)** index,
not air temperature alone. WBGT combines air temperature, humidity, radiant heat
and air movement, which is why a 40 °C dry day and a 36 °C humid day carry
different risk despite the lower reading.

WBGT is measured at each site every **15 minutes** during daylight hours and
every 60 minutes overnight, at the most exposed monitored point.

## 3. Activation thresholds

| WBGT | Level | Required measures |
|---|---|---|
| below 28 °C | NORMAL | Routine hydration points open |
| **28.0 – 29.9 °C** | **LEVEL 1 — cooling activated** | Misting fans on; hydration reminders broadcast; shaded rest areas opened |
| **30.0 – 31.9 °C** | **LEVEL 2** | All misting systems on; cold-water distribution at every entrance; medical crews pre-positioned at highest-density zones; work/rest cycle 45 min work / 15 min rest for outdoor staff |
| **32.0 – 32.9 °C** | **LEVEL 3** | Density thresholds tighten (section 4); mobile cooling units deployed; work/rest 30/30; movement advisories issued |
| **33.0 °C and above** | **LEVEL 4 — extreme** | Non-essential outdoor movement discouraged; consider phased diversion under SOP-CS-011; work/rest 15/45; Duty Operations Director informed |

**Cooling measures are triggered at a WBGT of 28 °C** — that is the Level 1
activation point. A WBGT reading of **32 °C places the site at Level 3**, which
requires mobile cooling units, a 30-minutes-work / 30-minutes-rest cycle for
outdoor staff, and the tightened density thresholds in section 4.

Thresholds are informed by the ISO 7243 WBGT framework for work/rest cycling.

## 4. Interaction with crowd density

Heat and density compound. A pilgrim in a dense crowd cannot reach shade or
water even when both are metres away, and radiant heat from surrounding bodies
raises effective exposure above the measured WBGT.

**At Level 3 and above, the HIGH escalation threshold in SOP-CS-004 section 3
drops from 80% to 70% utilisation.** Zones that would ordinarily be managed at
ELEVATED are managed at HIGH, with inflow throttling beginning earlier.

At Level 4, the CRITICAL threshold drops from 90% to **85%**.

## 5. Hydration and water provision

- Hydration points at every entrance and at intervals of no more than **150 m**
  along primary circulation routes.
- At Level 2 and above, cold water is distributed by roving staff inside dense
  zones, because pilgrims in those zones cannot reach fixed points.
- Water station servicing intervals and refill escalation are defined in
  **SOP-SAN-003**. A water station reported empty during Level 2 or above is
  raised at one priority higher than its SOP-OPS-001 default.

## 6. Recognition and first response

| Presentation | Signs | First action |
|---|---|---|
| Heat cramps | Muscle spasm, heavy sweating | Move to shade, oral rehydration |
| Heat exhaustion | Pale, clammy, dizzy, nauseated, still sweating | Shade, cooling, oral rehydration, monitor |
| **Heat stroke** | Hot skin, sweating may have stopped, **altered mental state**, collapse | **P1 MEDICAL** under SOP-MED-002; active cooling immediately; do not delay for transport |

Altered mental state is the distinguishing sign. Heat exhaustion with a
confused patient is treated as heat stroke until proven otherwise.

**Active cooling begins before transport, not after.** Cooling in the first
minutes determines outcome; transport time does not.

## 7. Staff protection

Outdoor staff follow the work/rest cycles in section 3. Supervisors track
cycles per crew and record compliance at handover under SOP-OPS-006. A crew that
has not taken its rest interval is withdrawn — a collapsed marshal becomes a
casualty inside the crowd they were managing.

## 8. Reporting

Every Level 2 activation and above is logged as an incident under
**SOP-OPS-020**, with WBGT readings, activation time, measures taken and any
heat-related medical requests raised during the period.
