# SOP-SEC-007 — Lost Person Procedure

## 1. Purpose

Defines the response to a reported missing person, with tighter timers for
children, elderly and vulnerable adults. SLA targets are in **SOP-OPS-001**:
LOST_PERSON is **P2 with a 10-minute response and 120-minute resolution target**,
or P3 (20 / 180) where the subject is a competent adult.

**A missing child, a missing elderly person, or a missing vulnerable adult is
always P2 or higher. It is never P3.**

## 2. The first three steps

When a missing person is reported, the receiving marshal performs these three
actions **in this order**, before anything else:

1. **Contain the reporter and record the description.** Do not let the reporting
   family member walk away to search — a second lost person doubles the problem.
   Record: name, age, height, clothing colours, footwear, distinguishing
   features, language spoken, wristband or group identifier, and the exact
   location and time last seen.
2. **Raise the request immediately as LOST_PERSON P2** in the operations
   platform, and broadcast the description on the sector radio channel. The
   broadcast happens within the first minute — before any search begins — so
   every marshal in the sector is watching while the search is still small.
3. **Seal the immediate area and search outward from the last known point.**
   Marshals at the zone's exits hold position and observe rather than joining
   the search. A child moves outward from where they were lost; staff who leave
   the exits to search inward let them past.

Only after these three steps are complete does the wider search escalate.

## 3. Timers

Timers run from the moment the request is raised, not from when the person was
last seen.

| Elapsed | Action |
|---|---|
| **0 min** | Description recorded, request raised P2, sector radio broadcast |
| **5 min** | Adjacent sectors notified; exits of adjacent zones observed |
| **10 min** | Sector Supervisor assumes coordination; reunification point notified |
| **20 min** | SOC Controller informed; site-wide description broadcast; CCTV review begins |
| **30 min** | Escalate to P1-equivalent handling; Duty Operations Director informed; treat as a security incident under SOP-OPS-020 |
| **60 min** | Civil Defence Liaison informed; external notification per SOP-OPS-020 |

For a child under 12, or any subject with a medical or cognitive vulnerability,
**every timer above halves**. The 30-minute escalation becomes 15 minutes.

## 4. Family reunification points

Each site operates staffed reunification points. A found person is escorted
there — never handed to a bystander who claims to be family, and never left at a
service desk.

| Site | Reunification point |
|---|---|
| HARAM_MAKKAH | Adjacent to King Abdulaziz Gate (zone HARAM_GATE_79) |
| MINA | Camp sector welfare office, Sector A |
| ARAFAT | Central services tent, Zone 1 |
| MUZDALIFAH | Northern services post |
| ALULA / DIRIYAH | Visitor centre information desk |

**Identity verification before release is mandatory.** The claiming adult must
provide a description that matches the record taken at step 1, and where the
subject is verbal, the subject must recognise them. If either check fails, the
subject stays at the reunification point and the matter is escalated to the
Security Directorate as a safeguarding concern under **SOP-OPS-020**. Releasing
a child to an unverified adult is the one irreversible failure in this
procedure.

## 5. Language

Reporters frequently do not speak Arabic or English. Marshals use the
multilingual phrase cards in **FAQ-001** and request an interpreter through the
SOC. The reporter's language is recorded on the request so the responding crew
is matched where possible. A description taken through a failed translation is
worse than no description, because it sends the search in a wrong direction.

## 6. Closure

A lost-person request is resolved only when the subject is reunited and the
reunification is recorded with the verifying staff member's identifier. A
subject who leaves the site independently before reunification is recorded as
`CANCELLED` with a note, never `RESOLVED`.

Every case reaching the 30-minute timer is reviewed at the next shift handover
under **SOP-OPS-006**.
