# SOP-CS-015 — Evacuation and Diversion / إجراءات الإخلاء والتحويل

## 1. Purpose

Defines the triggers for evacuating a zone, the routes and authority levels
involved, and how evacuation differs from diversion.

**Diversion** redirects *arriving* flow away from a zone; the people already
inside stay. **Evacuation** removes the people already inside. Diversion is
authorised under **SOP-CS-011**; evacuation requires the joint authority in
section 3 of this document.

## 2. Evacuation triggers

Any one of the following triggers evacuation:

- Crowd density reaching **4.0 persons/m²** and still rising despite inflow
  having been stopped (see **SOP-CS-004** section 2)
- Zone utilisation at or above **95%** with no viable diversion, or a receiving
  zone unavailable
- Fire, smoke or suspected structural failure
- Any credible threat to life directed at the zone
- Loss of both primary and secondary egress routes
- Civil Defence direction

## 3. Authority — إجراءات الإخلاء

**Evacuation is authorised jointly by the Duty Operations Director and the Civil
Defence Liaison.** A single authority is not sufficient. This is the only action
in the crowd-safety procedures requiring two signatures, because an evacuation
that begins and then stops is more dangerous than one never started.

**الإخلاء يتطلب موافقة مشتركة من مدير العمليات المناوب ومنسق الدفاع المدني.
لا تكفي موافقة جهة واحدة.**

Where delay would cost lives, any **SOC Controller** may order an **immediate
partial evacuation** of a single zone and must notify both authorities within
**60 seconds**. This emergency power covers one zone only and cannot be used to
evacuate a site.

## 4. Evacuation procedure — خطوات الإخلاء

1. **Stop all inflow** to the zone and to every zone feeding it.
2. **Open every egress route**, including contraflow lanes and routes normally
   reserved for staff or vehicles.
3. **Announce** in Arabic and English first, then in the languages listed in
   **FAQ-001**, using short repeated instructions naming the *direction* of
   travel rather than a route number. Pilgrims do not know route numbers.
4. **Marshals move with the flow, not against it.** Staff pushing upstream
   create the counterflow that turns an evacuation into a crush.
5. **Do not open a route that has not been confirmed clear** by a marshal at its
   far end. An announced route that dead-ends will kill people.
6. **Medical crews hold at the egress points**, not inside the zone, and triage
   as people emerge under **SOP-MED-002**.
7. **Confirm the zone is clear** by sweep, from the far end back toward the
   egress, before declaring evacuation complete.
8. **Hold the zone closed** until reopening conditions in section 6 are met.

### 4.1 Announcement content — محتوى الإعلان

Every announcement states: what to do, which direction, and nothing else.
"Move calmly toward the eastern exits" is usable. "Due to congestion in sector
four, please proceed in an orderly fashion to the designated assembly area" is
not — it is too long to repeat and too abstract to follow.

**يجب أن يتضمن الإعلان: ماذا تفعل، وفي أي اتجاه، ولا شيء آخر.**

**JAM_BRIDGE_L3 is the constrained case.** It has a single primary egress
direction, so an evacuation of JAM_BRIDGE_L3 must begin earlier than an
equivalent evacuation on the lower levels, and descent to JAM_BRIDGE_L2 is a
last resort — pushing level 3 onto level 2 raises level 2's density while it is
also evacuating.

Route status is confirmed at the start of every shift under **SOP-OPS-006**. A
blocked route is reported immediately under **SOP-OPS-020**.

## 6. Reopening

A zone reopens only when **all** of the following hold:

1. The triggering condition is resolved and confirmed by the Civil Defence
   Liaison.
2. All egress routes are confirmed clear and staffed.
3. Two consecutive utilisation readings below **70%**, at least **10 minutes**
   apart (hold point HP-4 in **SOP-CS-011**).
4. The Duty Operations Director authorises reopening.

## 7. Reporting

Every evacuation is severity **S1** under **SOP-OPS-020** and generates a full
incident report within 24 hours.
